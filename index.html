A Factual Overview Without Room for Debate

This site is about the best Final Fantasy title, Final Fantasy IX, within the Square Enix/Soft franchise. This is not conjecture. It is indisputable fact. Final Fantasy IX explores a world wrought with strife in which the protagonist must juggle the concepts of right, wrong, and societal perception. If you do not possess the ability to understand the depth that this story explores, you may be of the opinion that Final Fantasy VII is the best title in this franchise. This is objectively wrong.

When a kidnapping gone wrong brings a band of unlikely heroes together, a grand adventure unfolds across a world on the brink of war. Through political intrigue, ancient mysteries, and existential crises, Final Fantasy IX explores what it means to live, love, and find purpose in a world that doesn't always make sense. It is a story of identity, mortality, and the choices that define us—and it is, naturally, the greatest Final Fantasy ever made.
